# Haline Privacy Policy

Haline is a small ocean climate companion app. This policy explains what the app uses and what it does not do.

## Public NOAA data

Haline fetches public monthly global ocean temperature anomaly data from NOAA. This data is public climate data and is not about you.

## Purchases

Haline uses RevenueCat to process purchase status. RevenueCat may receive an anonymous app user ID and purchase or subscription status so the app can know which features are available.

## No ads, tracking, or sale of data

Haline does not show ads. Haline does not sell your data. Haline does not use third-party tracking for advertising.

## Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to haline.support@protonmail.com.
